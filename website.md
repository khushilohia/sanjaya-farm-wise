Sanjaya – Smart AI Farming Assistant (Kiosk + Mobile Platform)

Sanjaya is an AI-powered agricultural assistance platform designed to work seamlessly on both a village kiosk device and a mobile application. It helps farmers make informed decisions using real-time weather data, location intelligence, soil information, market insights, and AI-generated recommendations in simple, localized language.

AI Disease Detection for Cardamom & Ginger

Farmers can simply take a photo of the affected plant using the mobile app or kiosk camera.

How It Works

1. Farmer uploads or captures an image.
2. AI analyzes:
    * Leaves
    * Stem
    * Rhizomes
    * Plant discoloration
    * Spots and lesions
    * Wilting symptoms
3. The image is processed through a specialized Cardamom-Ginger disease model.
4. The detected disease and recommended actions are displayed in the farmer’s preferred language.

⸻

Diseases Supported

Cardamom

* Capsule Rot
* Rhizome Rot
* Leaf Blotch
* Damping Off
* Katte Viral Disease
* Clump Rot
* Shoot and Capsule Borer Infestation

Ginger

* Soft Rot
* Bacterial Wilt
* Rhizome Rot
* Leaf Spot
* Yellow Disease
* Shoot Borer Attack
* Nutrient Deficiency Symptoms

Smart Context-Aware Detection

The disease detection model is enhanced using:

* Current Location
* Temperature
* Humidity
* Rainfall Forecast
* Seasonal Conditions
* Farm History

For example, if heavy rainfall is occurring in a cardamom-growing region, the AI can increase its confidence for moisture-related diseases such as Capsule Rot or Rhizome Rot.

⸻

Disease History Tracking

The platform maintains a disease history for each farm:

* Previous Disease Occurrences
* Treatment Records
* Recovery Status
* Seasonal Disease Trends

This helps farmers prevent recurring outbreaks and allows agricultural officers to identify regional disease hotspots.
